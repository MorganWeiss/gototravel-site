Quick questions

Does it really work with TikTok and Instagram?

Yes. Share a reel to Go To Travel from the share menu, or paste a link or screenshot. We pull out the real places and check them before they're saved.

Which AI assistants can send me trips?

We're rolling out connections for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Meta Muse.

Do my friends need the app?

Yes, the free iPhone app. Your invite link shows them the trip first, then they join and vote in the app.

Who can see my trips? What do you do with my data?

You choose who sees each trip: only you, your crew, or everyone. We don't sell your personal data. You can download or delete your data anytime from the Privacy Center.

Is it free? Is there an Android app?

Early access is free. Go To Travel is on iPhone first.
