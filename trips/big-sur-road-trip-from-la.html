Big Sur road trip from LA

Highway 1 is open again. Some stops aren't.

  • 3 days, any winter weekend (checked for December 2026)
  • Families with kids about 4 and up; one car, two nights
  • Checked on Nov 10, 2026

Heads-up: what AI planners get wrong

  • Old info says Highway 1 is closed at Big Sur. It reopened at Regent's Slide on Jan 14, 2026; on Nov 10 Caltrans showed it open end to end, with one-way control at Rocky Creek Bridge (through Nov 30) and near Point Sur (through Dec 31).
  • No stopping for the classic Bixby Bridge photo: since August, Monterey County bans parking on Highway 1 from about 900 ft north to 700 ft south of the bridge, for 12 months.
  • The McWay Falls Overlook Trail is closed for a retaining wall project, and there's no beach access at McWay Falls.
  • Pfeiffer Big Sur reopened Oct 16 after the Timber Fire closure, but the Pfeiffer Falls trail stays closed; the Redwood Grove Boardwalk and River Path are open.
  • Hearst Castle is closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day; tours book up to 60 days ahead.
