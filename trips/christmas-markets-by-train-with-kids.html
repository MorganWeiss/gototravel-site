Christmas markets by train with kids

Three Christmas markets, two easy trains

  • 7 days, any week Nov 26 to Dec 13, 2026 (sample: Thu Dec 3 to Wed Dec 9)
  • Families with kids about 4 to 12 (notes for little ones and teens); fly into Vienna, home from Munich
  • Checked on Nov 10, 2026

Heads-up: what AI planners get wrong

  • Through Dec 12, construction in Germany thins the direct Railjet Xpress Vienna-Salzburg-Munich to every two hours, about 15 minutes slower. Extra ICE trains run.
  • Munich's free kids' workshop (Himmelswerkstatt, ages 6 to 12) needs registration, which opens 10 days before each date; it ends Dec 13.
  • Hellbrunn's famous trick fountains are closed until Mar 25, 2027. In Advent, go for the Advent market instead, which is closed Mondays (except Dec 21). Pony rides are gone.
  • Munich's market is closed Sunday Nov 22 and ends Dec 24 at 2 PM; the Residenz Christmas village ends Dec 22; Vienna's Rathausplatz market closes Dec 26.
