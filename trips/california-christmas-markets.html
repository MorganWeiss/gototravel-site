Santa Ynez Valley

Solvang's Julefest

Nov 27 to Jan 3 · Solvang Park and village

Pip: Light and music shows in Solvang Park, nightly 5 to 9.

Pip: Tree lighting Fri Dec 4 at 5:30, parade Sat Dec 5 at 11.

Julefest runs Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, and the Nisser Adventure clue sheets are at the Solvang Visitor Center.

  • Solvang Park in Solvang, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: solvangusa.com

Central Coast

Cambria Christmas Market

Cambria Pines Lodge · Nov 27 to Dec 31 · select nights, 5 to 9 PM

Pip: 3 million lights, a train ride and visits from Santa.

Pip: Sold-out nights don't sell at the door; weeknights are calmer.

At Cambria Pines Lodge, 2905 Burton Dr, on select dates Nov 27 to Dec 31, 2026, 5 to 9 PM: 3 million twinkling lights, a German-inspired artisan market, visits from Santa, a train ride, fire pits and live music. It's cashless.

Tickets come only from the market's own website, and the market itself suggests a weeknight to skip the crowds.

  • Cambria Pines Lodge in Cambria, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: cambriachristmasmarket.com

Bay Area · Daly City

Great Dickens Christmas Fair

5 weekends · Nov 21 to Dec 20, plus Nov 27

Victorian London inside the Cow Palace: over 4 acres of lanes, theatres and shops.

Open 10 AM to 6 PM.

Tickets are date-specific and sold online only.

Cow Palace, Daly City

Pip: A miniature steam train meets you at 'Victoria Station'.

Daily attendance is limited, and the Cow Palace doesn't sell tickets on site.

For kids, the Children's Tour of London starts with a miniature steam train at the Fair's 'Victoria Station', then a chimney-sweep training school where kids may receive a bronze collectible coin. (When we checked, the tour page linked to its 2025 guide.)

  • Cow Palace in Daly City, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: dickensfair.com

Orange County · Huntington Beach

Old World's Christmas Markt

Old World Village · Nov 15 to Jan 3

Pip: Icicle lights and green-and-red stalls, built into the village.

Pip: Hours aren't announced yet: check before you drive.

Old World Village is at 7561 Center Ave, Huntington Beach, home of the Old World Restaurant & Biergarten. Its Christmas Markt 2026 runs Nov 15, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; the hours were 'to be announced' when we checked.

  • Old World Village in Huntington Beach, CA, USA, open in Maps

Sources: simplygermanusa.com, oldworldhb.com
