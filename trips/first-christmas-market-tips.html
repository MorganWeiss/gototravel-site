Pip's tips · 1 of 8

Go at 4, not 7.

Pip: Dec 5 sunset: Vienna 4:01, Munich 4:21. Lights before bedtime.

On Dec 5, 2026 the sun sets at 4:01 PM in Vienna, 4:16 PM in Salzburg and 4:21 PM in Munich. Get there around 4 and the lights come on while you walk, well before bedtime.

Source: aa.usno.navy.mil

Pip's tips · 2 of 8

Order the kids' punch.

Pip: Stall 48 at Vienna's Rathausplatz pours only alcohol-free drinks.

Ask for Kinderpunsch, the kids' punch. At the Wiener Christkindlmarkt on Rathausplatz, stall No. 48, Wichtelglück & Zauberpunsch, serves organic hot and cold drinks, and all of them are alcohol-free.

  • Wiener Christkindlmarkt in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps

Source: christkindlmarkt.at

Pip's tips · 3 of 8

Mugs come with a deposit.

Pip: Return it to that stall for your coins. Want to keep it? Ask first.

Vienna's Christmas markets charge a deposit on punch mugs (the Häferlpfand), and you get it back when you return the mug. Keeping one isn't allowed unless the stall says yes, so ask if you'd like it as a souvenir.

Sources: wien.orf.at, correctiv.org

Pip's tips · 4 of 8

Bring a little cash.

Pip: Vienna's Rathausplatz market even has ATMs on site.

The Wiener Christkindlmarkt on Rathausplatz lists ATMs (Geldautomaten) on site, along with accessible toilets.

  • Wiener Christkindlmarkt in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps

Source: christkindlmarkt.at

Pip's tips · 5 of 8

Grab the free kids' quiz.

Pip: Sheets and pens: at the stand opposite Vienna's Tree of Hearts.

At the Wiener Christkindlmarkt, free quiz sheets and pens wait at the stand opposite the Tree of Hearts. The kids' area also has a 12 m carousel and a reindeer train.

  • Wiener Christkindlmarkt in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps

Source: christkindlmarkt.at

Pip's tips · 6 of 8

Toddler blades are free to borrow.

Pip: Vienna's kids' rink lends double blades (EU 21-25) and helmets with a ticket.

  • Wiener Christkindlmarkt in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps

Source: christkindlmarkt.at

Pip's tips · 7 of 8

Munich's kids' workshop is free.

Pip: Ages 6 to 12, Nov 26 to Dec 13. Sign-ups open 10 days ahead.

The Himmelswerkstatt in Munich's Neues Rathaus is free for ages 6 to 12, from Nov 26 to Dec 13, 2026. Sign up with the Kindermuseum München: sign-ups open 10 days ahead.

  • Münchner Christkindlmarkt in Munich, Germany, open in Maps

Source: muenchen.de

Pip's tips · 8 of 8

Salzburg has a petting zoo, too.

Pip: Hellbrunn's Advent: train and petting zoo, weekends 11 to 6. Closed Mondays.

Hellbrunner Adventzauber runs Nov 19 to Dec 24, 2026, and is closed Mondays except Dec 21. Its kids' Christmas world, with a Christmas train and a petting zoo, is open weekends and holidays, 11 AM to 6 PM.

  • Hellbrunner Adventzauber in Salzburg, Austria, open in Maps

Source: hellbrunneradventzauber.at
