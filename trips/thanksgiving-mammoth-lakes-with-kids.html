Heads-up: what AI planners get wrong

  • Ski lessons aren't a given: on Nov 9, 3 of 181 trails were open and there was no beginner terrain.
  • The Village Gondola was closed on Nov 9, and Mammoth's lodging page says it typically runs mid-December to mid-April.
  • Tioga Pass (Hwy 120) into Yosemite was still open Nov 6, but the Park Service says it closes with snow, usually in November, until late May or June.
  • Devils Postpile's season ended Oct 30, and the Lakes Basin road and Hwy 203 past Main Lodge are closed for winter.
  • Manzanar's visitor center is closed Tuesday to Thursday, which is why it's on Sunday's drive home, not Wednesday's.
  • June Mountain, Mammoth's family-friendly sister resort, doesn't open until Dec 19, 2026.
  • Thanksgiving dinners run on fixed seatings; The Lakefront opened bookings to the public on Nov 5. Apricity, the Westin's new restaurant, is on Mammoth's Thanksgiving list but still listed as coming soon, so we left it out.

Drive: Alabama Hills to Bishop. About 62 mi north on US-395.
