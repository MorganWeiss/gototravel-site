Three Christmas markets, two easy trains

7 days with kids · Vienna, Salzburg, Munich

For families with kids · Vienna · Salzburg · Munich

Checked Nov 10, 2026

Pip: Both trains are direct, and both take under three hours.

Photo: Image by www.traveljunction.com, CC BY-SA 2.0

‹ All plans you can copy

Seven days, three cities, two direct trains: Vienna for 2 nights, about 2 h 25 by Railjet to Salzburg for 2 nights, then about 2 h by train to Munich for 2 nights. Fly into Vienna and home from Munich, and start on a Thursday so Salzburg lands on the weekend. Every date was checked on Nov 10, 2026.

  • 7 days, any week Nov 26 to Dec 13, 2026

Notes with the little globe are from Pip, our trip planner: the why behind each pick.

Vienna · nights 1 and 2

Wiener Christkindlmarkt

City Hall · Nov 13 to Dec 26

Pip: A 12 m carousel and a kids' rink in the park next door.

Pip: Schönbrunn's palace market lets kids skate free.

The Rathausplatz market is open Nov 13 to Dec 26, 2026, daily 10 AM to 10 PM (Dec 24 until 6:30 PM). In Rathauspark next door, the kids' Christmas world has a 12 m double-decker carousel, a reindeer train, a Ferris wheel and a kids' rink with free double-blade skates (sizes 21 to 25) and helmets with a ticket.

Schönbrunn Palace's market runs Nov 6, 2026 to Jan 6, 2027: 10 AM to 9 PM until Dec 23, 10 AM to 4 PM on Dec 24, and 10 AM to 7 PM from Dec 25. Kids skate free on its Glice rink, and there's a carousel, a Ferris wheel, a kids' railway and a craft workshop.

  • Wiener Christkindlmarkt in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps
  • Weihnachtsmarkt Schloss Schönbrunn in Vienna, Austria, open in Maps

Sources: christkindlmarkt.at, wien.info

Salzburg · nights 3 and 4

Salzburger Christkindlmarkt

Domplatz · Nov 19 to Jan 1 · daily

Pip: Hellbrunn's Advent has a train and a petting zoo on weekends.

Pip: Start on a Thursday and Salzburg lands on the weekend.

The Domplatz market runs Nov 19, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027: Monday to Thursday 10 AM to 8:30 PM, Friday 10 AM to 9 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 9 PM, and Sundays and holidays 9 AM to 8:30 PM.

Hellbrunner Adventzauber runs Nov 19 to Dec 24, 2026: Tuesday to Friday 1 to 8 PM, weekends and holidays 10 AM to 8 PM, closed Mondays except Dec 21. Its kids' Christmas world, with a train and a petting zoo, is open weekends, 11 AM to 6 PM.

  • Salzburger Christkindlmarkt in Salzburg, Austria, open in Maps
  • Hellbrunner Adventzauber in Salzburg, Austria, open in Maps

Sources: christkindlmarkt.co.at, hellbrunneradventzauber.at

Munich · nights 5 and 6

Münchner Christkindlmarkt

Marienplatz · Nov 20 to Dec 24 · closed Nov 22

Pip: Advent music from City Hall's balcony, Sun to Thu at 5:15.

Pip: St. Nicholas visits the Residenz village Dec 6 and 7 from 5.

The Marienplatz market runs Nov 20 to Dec 24, 2026, closed Nov 22: Monday to Saturday 10 AM to 9 PM, Sunday 10 AM to 8 PM, and Dec 24 10 AM to 2 PM. The free Himmelswerkstatt for ages 6 to 12 runs Nov 26 to Dec 13; sign-ups open 10 days ahead.

The Weihnachtsdorf in the Residenz's Kaiserhof is open Nov 16 to Dec 22, daily 11 AM to 9 PM, with a free fairy-tale forest. St. Nicholas visits Dec 6 and 7 from 5 PM.

  • Münchner Christkindlmarkt in Munich, Germany, open in Maps
  • Weihnachtsdorf im Kaiserhof der Residenz in Munich, Germany, open in Maps

Source: muenchen.de

The route

Two trains. That's all.

Pip: Fly into Vienna, home from Munich: no backtracking.

  1. 1Vienna · 2 nights
  2. about 2 h 25 · Railjet
  3. 2Salzburg · 2 nights
  4. about 2 h · train
  5. 3Munich · 2 nights

Vienna to Salzburg: two Railjets an hour, and the Railjet Xpress takes 2 h 25 min. Munich to Salzburg takes from 1 h 40 min.

Until Dec 12, 2026, works in Germany make the direct Railjet Xpress Vienna to Salzburg to Munich (every two hours, as in the regular timetable) about 15 minutes slower, and two extra ICE pairs run.

Sources: salzburg.info, kundeninformation-pv.oebb.at
