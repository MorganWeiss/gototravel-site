Thanksgiving in the mountains, 5 hours from LA

Mammoth Lakes with kids · Nov 25 to 29

For families with kids · From LA · 4 nights

Checked Nov 10, 2026

Five days in the Eastern Sierra over Thanksgiving week, built on what's open in late November: tubing, a summit gondola, a free tree lighting and one road there, US-395. Every stop was checked on Nov 10, 2026.

Notes with the little globe are from Pip, our trip planner: the why behind each pick.

Wed Nov 25 · the drive up 395

Alabama Hills

Granite arches below the Sierra, west of Lone Pine

Pip: There's a porta potty at the Mobius Arch trailhead.

Pip: Lunch in Bishop: Schat's, home of Sheepherder Bread since 1938.

The Alabama Hills National Scenic Area is west of Lone Pine, by Whitney Portal Road and Movie Road.

In Bishop, Erick Schat's Bakkery bakes its Original Sheepherder Bread (since 1938) and has a sandwich bar.

  • Alabama Hills in Lone Pine, CA, USA, open in Maps
  • Erick Schat's Bakkery in Bishop, CA, USA, open in Maps

Sources: blm.gov, schatsbakery.com

Thu Nov 26 · Thanksgiving

Tubes, then turkey

Woolly's Tube Park · daily Nov 20 to 29, weather permitting

Pip: Tube riders must be 42 inches or taller.

Pip: Dinner seatings at Mountainside: 2:30, 5 or 7:30.

Woolly's Tube Park is scheduled daily Nov 20 to 29, 2026, weather and conditions permitting, and a free Red Line shuttle runs every 20 minutes.

Mountainside Bar & Grill's Thanksgiving dinner on Nov 26 has carving stations.

  • Woolly's Tube Park in Mammoth Lakes, CA, USA, open in Maps
  • Mountainside Bar & Grill in Mammoth Lakes, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: mammothmountain.com

Fri Nov 27 · summit and lights

Up to 11,053 feet

Panorama Gondola · daily from Nov 13, weather permitting

Pip: Two kids 12 and under ride free with each paying adult.

Pip: Village tree lighting: Santa and Woolly light it at 8. Free.

Scenic gondola rides go up to the 11,053-foot summit daily from Nov 13, 2026, weather permitting.

The Holiday Tree Lighting in The Village runs 6 to 8:30 PM on Fri Nov 27.

  • Panorama Gondola in Mammoth Lakes, CA, USA, open in Maps
  • The Village at Mammoth in Mammoth Lakes, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: mammothmountain.com

Sat Nov 28 · a slow day

Biscuits, a pool, bowling

Bird and the Biscuit · the inn's pool · Rock 'n' Bowl

Pip: Ski lessons only if beginner terrain opens: check the report.

Pip: The inn's heated pool and hot tubs run 8 AM to 10 PM.

Bird and the Biscuit is closed Wednesdays and open 8 AM to 8 PM on Saturdays. Mammoth Rock 'N' Bowl opens at 4 PM.

Ski school takes kids 3 and up who are potty-trained, and kids 4 and under ski free with a ticket. But on Nov 9, 3 of 181 trails were open and there was no beginner terrain, so check the Mountain Report before you count on lessons.

  • Bird and the Biscuit in Mammoth Lakes, CA, USA, open in Maps

Sources: mammothmountain.com, visitmammoth.com

Sun Nov 29 · the drive home

Manzanar

National Historic Site · on US-395 near Independence

Pip: Its visitor center opens Fri to Mon: that's why it's Sunday.

Pip: Block 14's rebuilt barracks are open daily, 9 to 4:15.

Manzanar's visitor center is open Friday to Monday, 9 AM to 4:30 PM, and closed Tuesday to Thursday. The grounds are open sunrise to sunset, and Block 14 is open daily, 9 AM to 4:15 PM.

  • Manzanar National Historic Site in Independence, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: nps.gov

The route

One road: US-395

Pip: About 5 hours without stops. Carry chains in winter.

  1. 1Los Angeles · start
  2. 213 mi
  3. 2Alabama Hills · stretch stop
  4. 62 mi
  5. 3Bishop · bakery lunch
  6. 43 mi

Mammoth is about 5 hours north of Los Angeles. In winter the Lakes Basin road and Highway 203 past Main Lodge close, and you should carry chains.

Sources: router.project-osrm.org, mammothmountain.com, visitmammoth.com

Where we'd stay

Mammoth Mountain Inn

Our editorial pick

  • Across from Main Lodge, steps from ski school and the gondola.
  • Heated pool and hot tubs, 8 AM to 10 PM, and a game room.
  • Thanksgiving dinner is upstairs at Mountainside.

10400 Minaret Rd, Mammoth Lakes

Editorial picks. Nobody paid to be here.

Pip: Rather cook the turkey? The Village Lodge has condos with kitchens.

Check-in is at 4 PM.

The Village Lodge has studios to 3-bedroom condos with full kitchens, a heated pool and a game room.
