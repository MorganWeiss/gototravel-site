New Year's in NYC with kids

New Year's in NYC with kids, minus Times Square

  • Wed Dec 30, 2026 to Fri Jan 1, 2027
  • Families with kids about 3 to 15 (notes for strollers and teens)
  • Checked on Nov 10, 2026

Heads-up: what AI planners get wrong

  • Times Square at midnight isn't a kid plan: viewing areas open at 3 PM, there are no portable restrooms, and if you leave your spot you can't get back in.
  • The Met is closed on Wednesdays and on New Year's Day, so on a Dec 30 to Jan 1 trip, Thursday Dec 31 is your only Met day.
  • Bryant Park's free skating still needs a reserved entry time; bring-your-own-skates slots open a week out, and skate aids are free before 10:30 AM.
  • Prospect Park's free fireworks need an RSVP; the Statue of Liberty is open on Jan 1 (it closes only on Thanksgiving and Dec 25).
  • Kids under 44 inches ride the subway free, up to three per paying adult.

Day 1: Skates, a show and the tree

Walk: Bryant Park to Ellen's Stardust Diner. Midtown, about 10 blocks north.

Walk: Ellen's to Radio City. A few blocks east.

Walk: Radio City to Rockefeller Center. Across the street, practically.

  1. 1

    Bryant Park Winter Village rink

    9:00 AM · Bryant Park, Midtown Manhattan

    Free-admission skating; skate aids free before 10:30 AM

    With kids: Penguin aids for beginners under 4 feet; kids under 14 skate with an adult.

    Reserve an entry time on bryantpark.org; rental skates cost extra. Open daily, weather permitting.

    Open in Maps
  2. 2

    Ellen's Stardust Diner

    12:00 PM · 1650 Broadway, New York, NY 10019

    The waitstaff sings from open to close, 7 AM to midnight

    With kids: Stroller parking out front on Broadway, with free locks (space is limited).

    No reservations; expect a line.

    Open in Maps
  3. 3

    Radio City Christmas Spectacular

    2:00 PM · 1260 6th Ave, New York, NY

    The Rockettes' 2026 run goes through Jan 4, 2027

    With kids: For little ones, pick the earliest show of the day.

    Book on rockettes.com/christmas; we didn't check Dec 30 show times.

    Open in Maps
  4. 4

    Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree

    5:00 PM · Rockefeller Plaza, New York, NY

    Lit on Dec 2, with the rink below it (open since Oct 10)

    2026 visiting hours hadn't been announced when we checked.

    Open in Maps

Day 2: The Met, early dinner, fireworks

Subway or walk: The Met to Hotel. Rest in the afternoon. Stay clear of the Times Square viewing areas, which open at 3 PM.

Subway: Hotel to Junior's Brooklyn. Kids under 44 inches ride free (up to three per paying adult).

Subway: Junior's to Grand Army Plaza. Plan the trip with the MTA app before you go.

  1. 5

    The Metropolitan Museum of Art

    10:00 AM · 1000 Fifth Ave, New York, NY 10028

    Open 10 AM to 5 PM: the only Met day on this trip

    Closed Wednesdays and on Jan 1.

    Open in Maps
  2. 6

    Junior's Restaurant & Bakery

    5:00 PM · 386 Flatbush Ave Ext, Brooklyn, NY 11201

    Early dinner and cheesecake in Downtown Brooklyn

    Thursdays 7 AM to 11 PM; reserve or join the waitlist online.

    Open in Maps
  3. 7

    Prospect Park fireworks, Grand Army Plaza

    10:00 PM · Grand Army Plaza, Brooklyn, NY 11238

    Free New Year's Eve fireworks, 10 PM to 12:30 AM

    With kids: Little ones asleep by then? The Times Square webcast starts at 6 PM; watch the ball drop from bed.

    Free; reserve a spot through prospectpark.org/nye.

    Open in Maps

Day 3: New Year's Day: what's open

Subway: Hotel to Battery Park ferry. Ferries leave from Battery Park in Lower Manhattan.

Ferry (statue city cruises): Battery Park to Liberty Island. The only way onto the island.

  1. 8

    Statue of Liberty

    10:30 AM · Liberty Island, New York, NY 10004

    Open Jan 1; it closes only on Thanksgiving and Dec 25

    With kids: Last entry into the statue is 3:30 PM.

    Ferry only, with Statue City Cruises from Battery Park.

    Open in Maps
