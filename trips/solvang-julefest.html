Christmas in Europe, 2.5 hours from LA

Solvang's Julefest · Nov 27 to Jan 3

For families with kids · Solvang · 2.5 hours from LA

Checked Nov 10, 2026

Pip: Parking is free all over the village, hotels too.

Photo: Ligocsicnarf89, CC BY 4.0

‹ All plans you can copy

Solvang's Julefest runs Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027: light shows every night, a nisse hunt for the kids, and æbleskiver. Every date here was checked on Nov 10, 2026 on Solvang's official visitor site.

  • Solvang's Julefest: Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

Getting there

125 miles up the coast, about 2½ hours from downtown LA (US-101 to exit 140A, then 3 miles east on 246). No car? Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner to Santa Barbara, then the Amtrak bus into the village (two a day, sold with your train ticket).

Source: solvangusa.com

Eat this

Æbleskiver

Solvang Restaurant · Æbleskivehuset

Pip: Round Danish pancakes, powdered sugar, raspberry jam.

Pip: Solvang Restaurant calls itself the home of Arne's Aebleskiver.

Solvang's official visitor site says Solvang Restaurant and Æbleskivehuset both serve them fresh. Solvang Restaurant is at 1672 Copenhagen Dr. Neither posts its hours where we could check, so look before you go.

Where we'd stay

Alisal Ranch

Our editorial pick

  • A 10,500-acre guest ranch for families, couples and multi-generational trips.
  • Horseback riding on 50 miles of trails.
  • About 3 miles from Solvang Park.

1054 Alisal Rd, Solvang

Editorial picks. Nobody paid to be here.

Pip: Riding with little ones? Ask the ranch about ages first.

Alisal Ranch is 2.8 miles by road from Solvang Park, and it lists December 2026 events. It doesn't publish riding ages, so ask before you plan a ride with little ones.

Sources: alisalranch.com, router.project-osrm.org
