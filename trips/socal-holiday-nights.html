Eight holiday nights out from LA to San Diego, in date order, with every date taken from the event's own site on Nov 10, 2026.

Free to watch: The Grove's tree lighting, December Nights, the Bay Parade of Lights and the Newport boat parade. Ticketed: L.A. Zoo Lights and Enchanted. Jungle Bells comes with San Diego Zoo admission.

Mon Nov 16 · Los Angeles

Christmas at The Grove

25th tree lighting · free · space is limited

Pip: Tree lighting, first snowfall, drone show, fireworks, Santa.

Pip: It's filmed for TV, so expect a crowd.

It's complimentary and open to the public, and space is limited. The start time wasn't posted yet when we checked.

  • The Grove in Los Angeles, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: thegrovela.com

Dec 4 and 5 · San Diego

December Nights, Balboa Park

Fri 4 to 10 PM · Sat noon to 10 PM · free

Pip: San Diego's biggest free holiday festival.

Pip: Saturday starts at noon: easier with little ones.

  • Balboa Park in San Diego, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: sandiego.gov

Sundays Dec 13 and 20 · San Diego

Bay Parade of Lights

5:30 PM · about 80 lit boats · free to watch

Pip: More than 100,000 people line the bay: go early.

Pip: 2026 theme: Liberty Lights the Bay.

This is the 56th parade, and its full 2026 theme is Liberty Lights the Bay - Cheers to 250 Years.

  • San Diego Bay in San Diego, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: sdparadeoflights.org

Dec 16 to 20 · Newport Beach

Christmas Boat Parade

Nightly, about 6:30 PM · 118th year

Pip: Watch free from Marina Park, which has a playground.

Pip: Opening night, Dec 16: fireworks off the pier at about 6:15.

The boats set off from the tip of Lido Isle each night, Wed Dec 16 to Sun Dec 20, 2026.

Marina Park, at 1600 W Balboa Blvd, is the largest public viewing area, with a nautical-themed playground.

  • Marina Park in Newport Beach, CA, USA, open in Maps

Source: christmasboatparade.com

Lights worth a late bedtime
