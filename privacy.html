Privacy Policy

Updated [DATE OF LAUNCH]

Go To Travel ("we", "us") runs gototravelapp.com and the Go To Travel iPhone app. This policy covers the website: the waitlist, the traveler quiz, our emails, the contact form and the Privacy Center. The app shows its own privacy details in the App Store and in the app.

The short version. We collect your email and the answers you choose to give us, to email you about Go To Travel and to set up the app for you. We don't sell your personal data. Ads cookies load only if you say yes. You can change your email choices, download your data or delete it anytime in the Privacy Center, no password needed.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist, ask for a free kit or tips, or contact us.
  • Quiz answers, if you take the traveler quiz: who you travel with, your travel style and pace, what you love, and your traveler type.
  • Kids' answers entered by a parent, if you pick Family: an age range, what your kids are into and food needs. You enter these yourself; we don't ask for kids' names on the website. The site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
  • Your dream board: the postcards you heart on the home page.
  • Referral info: your personal invite code, the code you arrived with (if any), and how many friends joined with your link.
  • Proof of consent: when you signed up, which version of our sign-up wording you saw, and your country (from your connection, not your exact location).
  • Contact messages: your name, email and message when you use the contact form.
  • Technical info: a one-way hash of your IP address (never the address itself), your browser type, and the campaign link you arrived from (UTM tags), used to stop spam and see which channels work.

How we use it

  • To send the emails you asked for: your welcome email, the kit, Friday trip ideas, launch news and beta invites.
  • To set up the app for you, so your quiz answers, kids' answers and dream board are waiting when you sign in with the same email.
  • To run the waitlist, including "skip the line" invites.
  • To reply to you.
  • To keep the site working, stop spam, and understand which pages and campaigns work.

Email and unsubscribing

Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link and a link to your email preferences. Unsubscribing takes effect right away. We'll still send emails you need, like sign-in links, crew invites and trip changes you ask for. In the US we use single opt-in with a welcome email. Selling and sharing (California and similar laws)

We don't sell your personal data for money. If you turn on Ads cookies, the TikTok and Meta pixels may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out anytime with Do not sell or share my personal information or with Global Privacy Control.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase stores your signup and messages.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • TikTok and Meta, only if you turn on Ads cookies.
  • AI assistants you connect. In the app you can connect an assistant such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Meta Muse. Trip data goes only to the assistants you connect, and you can disconnect them anytime.

Service providers process data for us under contract. We'd also share data if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

In the Privacy Center you can change email preferences, unsubscribe from everything, download your data, and delete it. Deleting takes two steps: we email you a confirmation link, and nothing is deleted until you click it. Your app account is deleted in the app under Profile.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may also have rights to access, correct, port or delete your data, to object or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using them. Email us at the address below for anything the Privacy Center doesn't cover.
