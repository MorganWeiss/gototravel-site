Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Go To Travel ("we", "us") runs gototravelapp.com and the Go To Travel iPhone app. This policy covers both: the website (the waitlist, the traveler quiz, our emails, the contact form and the Privacy Center) and the app.

The short version. We collect what you choose to give us, to plan your trips, share them with your crew and email you about Go To Travel. We don't sell your personal data, the app has no ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or sites. Pip, the app's guide, uses AI from Google and Anthropic, and the app asks before sending them anything. Delete your app account anytime in the app (Profile, then Delete account). Manage or delete your website data in the Privacy Center, no password needed.

What we collect on the website

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist, ask for a free kit or tips, or contact us.
  • Quiz answers, if you take the traveler quiz: who you travel with, your travel style and pace, what you love, and your traveler type.
  • Kids' answers entered by a parent, if you pick Family: an age range, what your kids are into and food needs. You enter these yourself; we don't ask for kids' names on the website. Food needs such as nut-free or gluten-free can say something about health, so we keep them only because you chose to tell us, use them only to suggest places to eat and tailor your kit, never for ads, and you can delete them anytime in the Privacy Center. The site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
  • Your dream board: the postcards you heart on the home page.
  • Your app look, if you pick one after signing up: dark or light.
  • Referral info: your personal invite code, the code you arrived with (if any), and how many friends joined with your link.
  • Proof of consent: when you signed up, which version of our sign-up wording you saw, and your country (from your connection, not your exact location).
  • Contact messages: your name, email and message when you use the contact form.
  • Technical info: a one-way hash of your IP address (never the address itself), your browser type, and the campaign link you arrived from (UTM tags), used to stop spam and see which channels work.

What the app collects

  • Your account: your name and email address, from sign-up, Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email, we get a relay address instead of your own.
  • Your trips: where and when you're going, who's coming, your pace, budget and interests, the hotel you add, the plan Pip makes, your swaps, votes and notes, packing lists, budgets, expenses and receipts you scan.
  • Kids' details a parent adds: a first name, age and interests, and only if you choose, food needs and accessibility, mobility or special-needs notes. Notes like these can say something about health, so we keep them only because you chose to add them, use them only to plan suitable stops and places to eat, never for ads, and you can edit or delete them anytime in the app. Kids don't have accounts.
  • Your saves: the places, links, captions and screenshots you share into the app (for example from Instagram or TikTok) and your bucket list. When you share a link, the app reads that post's text to find the places in it.
  • Photos you add: trip-note photos, receipt photos and screenshots.
  • Your town, if you allow location: your phone works out your town, to suggest your home city and things to do near you this weekend. Your exact position isn't sent to us.
  • Your crew: when you plan a trip with others, they see your name, your picks, your votes and the notes and photos you add to that trip.
  • Messages to Pip: what you ask Pip in the chat.
  • Your membership, if you subscribe: your plan and whether it's active, from Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
  • How you use Pip: your saves, swaps and votes from the last 90 days, which Pip uses to suggest better stops (you can see them in the app under "What Go To Travel knows about me"), a history of changes to each trip, and whether Pip could read each post you shared.

The app doesn't collect your contacts, browsing history, precise location or advertising identifier, and it has no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Pip and AI

Pip uses AI from Google (Gemini) to plan trips, read the posts, screenshots and receipts you share, and suggest stops, and from Anthropic (Claude) to answer you in Pip's chat. The app asks before sending anything to them, and you can switch Pip's AI help off anytime in Profile. Without it, the rest of the app keeps working.

What we send: your trip details (where, when, who's coming, kids' ages and interests, and the food or accessibility notes you added), your messages to Pip, and the text or image of a post or receipt you share. We don't send kids' names. Our providers process this only to give you answers, under terms that don't let them train their models on it. Pip is an AI guide and can get things wrong, so check important details before you go.

Places, maps and weather

To show places, ratings and photos, the app looks up the place name and city with Google Maps Platform. Maps on iPhone come from Apple Maps. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, which receives the location of the place you're going. Some destination photos come from Unsplash.

How we use it

  • To send the emails you asked for: your welcome email, the kit, Friday trip ideas, launch news and beta invites.
  • To set up the app for you, so your quiz answers, kids' answers, dream board and app look are waiting when you sign in with the same email.
  • To run the waitlist, including "skip the line" invites.
  • To reply to you.
  • To keep the site working, stop spam, and understand which pages and campaigns work.
  • In the app: to plan and run your trips, share them with the crew you invite, personalize Pip's suggestions, send the notifications you turn on (like weekend ideas and "your trip is ready"), run your membership, help you when you write to us, and keep the app safe.

Why we're allowed to use it (EU and UK)

  • Your consent for marketing email (the welcome email, your kit, Friday ideas and launch news), for optional cookies and pixels, for sending your trip details to our AI providers, and, as explicit consent, for kids' food, accessibility and health-related notes. You can withdraw it anytime: unsubscribe, change cookie settings, switch off Pip's AI help in the app, or delete those notes in the app or the Privacy Center.
  • Doing what you asked for running your waitlist spot, sending the kit you requested, answering your messages, and providing the app: your account, your trips, your crew and your membership.
  • Our legitimate interests for keeping the site secure, stopping spam and understanding which pages work. You can object anytime by emailing us.

Where your data is stored

Our providers store and process data in the United States. When data comes from the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on their data processing terms, which include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum).

Email and unsubscribing

Every marketing email ends with an Unsubscribe button, a link to your email preferences and our postal address, and mail apps can unsubscribe you in one click. Unsubscribing takes effect right away. We'll still send emails you need, like sign-in links, crew invites and trip changes you ask for. In the US and most other places we use single opt-in. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask you to confirm first: you're on the list only after you tap the link in our confirmation email, and we send nothing else until then. Our postal address: Axiom AI Labs LLC, 2710 Centerville Rd #200, Wilmington, DE 19808.

Selling and sharing (California and similar laws)

We don't sell your personal data for money. If you turn on Ads cookies, the TikTok and Meta pixels may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out anytime with Do not sell or share my personal information or with Global Privacy Control.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase stores your account, trips and photos, and runs sign-in.
  • Google: Gemini plans trips and reads what you share (only with your OK), Google Maps Platform looks up places, and Google sign-in if you use it.
  • Anthropic runs Pip's chat (only with your OK).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and App Store purchases if you subscribe.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of your membership.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather, and Unsplash some photos.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • TikTok and Meta, only if you turn on Ads cookies on the website.
  • Your crew: the people you invite to a trip see what you add to it.
  • AI assistants you connect (a beta feature): if you connect your own assistant, trip data goes only to the assistants you connect, and you can disconnect them anytime.

Service providers process data for us under contract. We'd also share data if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

In the Privacy Center you can change email preferences, unsubscribe from everything, download your data, and delete it. Deleting takes two steps: we email you a confirmation link, and nothing is deleted until you click it. Your app account is deleted in the app: Profile, then Delete account. That deletes your account, trips, saves, notes, photos and kids' profiles. On a trip you planned with others, the plan stays for them and passes to the person who joined first. If you subscribe through Apple, cancel the subscription in your Apple account settings too. For a copy of your app data, email us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may also have rights to access, correct, port or delete your data, to object or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using them. Email us at the address below for anything the Privacy Center doesn't cover.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist signups: until you delete them, or until we close the waitlist and you haven't created an app account.
  • Contact messages: as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Browser storage: on your device until you clear it.
  • App account data: until you delete your account. We remove it from our live systems right away and from backups within 30 days.
  • Membership records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as tax and accounting rules require.

Children

Go To Travel is for adults planning trips. It isn't directed at children under 13, and children don't have accounts. A parent may add their kids' first names, ages and needs to plan a trip, and can change or delete them anytime in the app.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
